os!strike
=========

osu! on a curved screen floating in space, played in first person with a
Counter-Strike crosshair and weapons.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Open os!strike.exe (keep it in this folder, next to "maps", "skins" and "sounds").
2. PLAY -> pick a song -> click it to see the difficulties -> click again to play.
3. Aim with the mouse and shoot the circles: left click, Z, X or right click.
   Sliders: hold and follow the ball. Spinners: hold and spin around the centre.

KEYS
----
ESC          pause (continue / retry / options / quit)
R            retry
SPACE        skip the intro
1 / 2 / 3    AK-47 / USP-S / Desert Eagle   (Q = last weapon, mouse wheel = cycle)
F1           mods (in the song select)
TAB          change skin (in the song select)

YOUR OWN CONTENT
----------------
maps\    osu! beatmaps. Drop .osz files here (they are extracted automatically)
         or copy song folders from osu!\Songs.
skins\   osu! skins. Drop .osk files or skin folders here.
sounds\  custom hitsounds (.wav, .ogg or .mp3).
See the README.txt inside each folder.

Settings and scores are saved in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\os!trike
(delete that folder to reset everything).

REQUIREMENTS
------------
Windows 10/11 64-bit, a graphics card with DirectX 12 or Vulkan.
If the game runs slowly: Options -> VIDEO -> ADVANCED GRAPHICS -> PRESET "Low",
or lower RENDER SCALE and use AMD FSR.
