os!strike SKINS
===============

Put osu! skins here: .osk files or skin folders. .osk files are extracted
automatically. Choose the skin in Options -> OSU! -> SKIN, or press TAB in the
song select. "os!strike (default)" is the built-in look.

Skins change the hit circles, sliders, approach circles, numbers, cursor,
pause/fail screens and (if enabled) the hitsounds.
